Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Central Government licensed, Section 8 non-profit based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. We believe in the power of collective action to build a compassionate, inclusive and empowered society.

80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered NITI Aayog Registered ISO 9001:2015 Section 8 NGO

Our Journey

Milestones

Sept 23, 2020

Foundation Established

InAmigos Foundation was founded by Mr. Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh with a vision to empower the underprivileged.

2021

Key Certifications Achieved

Received 80G & 12A certifications, CSR-1 registration, and NITI Aayog registration — enabling tax-exempt donations and national-level collaborations.

2022

ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Achieved the prestigious IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification, demonstrating commitment to high-quality standards in operations and service.

2023–2025

National Expansion

Expanded operations to 28 states, growing the volunteer network to 200+ members and serving over 50,000 beneficiaries across India.

2025

Events & Campaigns

Organized World Water Day, International Day of Happiness, and Women in Science campaigns — driving community awareness nationwide.
